# VR Library

3D cubes of the learner's space (z, ż, P), to explore on a desktop or in a VR headset.
Each cube shows the transition bands the SKA learner registers from one ECG record.

## Cubes

PhysioNet Fantasia Database, healthy subjects at supine rest, ECG, 250 Hz. Open a cube and press **Enter VR** in the headset browser.

Three cubes per record, at scale 25: the cube (P), the same with the steps into or out of a missing wave tagged (×), and the cube with Δt, the time since the previous wave, as the vertical axis.
